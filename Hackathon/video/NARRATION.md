# TideWatch Narration v0.1

> **语气**：冷静、克制、有重量，不使用电影预告片式吼腔。
> **语速**：英文约 115-125 WPM。
> **目标时长**：约 2:20-2:30，数据画面之间保留呼吸。

## English Voice-over

```text
[0:00 - silence, distant tide]

Most AI systems make a prediction, then move on.
The answer remains.
The lesson disappears.

TideWatch was born to read the market:
price, momentum, money, news,
and the market regime around them.

But it did one thing differently.
Every signal became a promise.
Five, ten, and twenty trading days later,
the market came back to grade it.

Its first lesson came from contradiction.
Two weak bullish calls fell 9.3 percent.
A stronger call, facing the same conflict, broke through.

Lesson one:
when evidence conflicts and conviction is weak,
do nothing.
A mistake became a guardrail.

More signals returned.
A so-called slightly bullish zone
had failed four times out of four,
losing 6.58 percent on average.

TideWatch stopped softening bad answers.
It removed the answer.

Then came the most unsettling lesson.
Near-perfect scores were right only 56.3 percent of the time,
while the lower band reached 89.5 percent.

Confidence was no longer taken at face value.
Reversals were penalized.
Noisy factors lost weight.
Stronger evidence gained it.

With 135 signals,
the hidden variable finally surfaced.
High-confidence calls reached 72.1 percent.
Low-confidence calls fell to 47.8.

So TideWatch learned its hardest lesson:
when confidence is low,
do not force a direction.

Today, every analysis enters the same loop:
predict,
remember,
verify,
reflect,
and return stronger.

It was built to read the market.
But only the market could teach it how.

TideWatch.
Born to predict.
Taught by the tide.
```

## 中文字幕

```text
[0:00 - 黑场，远处潮声]

大多数 AI 做出预测，然后继续向前。
答案留下，
教训却消失了。

TideWatch 生来用于理解市场：
价格、动量、资金、消息，
以及它们所处的市场体制。

但它多做了一件事。
每个信号都是一次承诺。
五个、十个、二十个交易日后，
市场终会回来批改。

第一课来自矛盾。
两个勉强看多的判断，五日下跌 9.3%。
更强的判断，却穿越了同样的冲突。

第一课：
证据冲突、判断不强时，
不交易。
一次错误，变成一道护栏。

更多信号完成回填。
所谓“弱多”区间，
四次判断，四次全错，
平均下跌 6.58%。

TideWatch 不再美化不可靠的答案。
它直接删除了这个答案。

接着，是最反直觉的一课。
接近满分的判断只有 56.3% 正确，
较低区间却达到 89.5%。

系统不再照单全收自己的自信。
方向翻转被惩罚，
噪声因子失去权重，
更可靠的证据得到加强。

当信号增长到 135 条，
隐藏的变量终于浮现。
高置信度达到 72.1%，
低置信度只有 47.8%。

TideWatch 学会了最难的一课：
没有把握时，
不要强行给出方向。

今天，每次分析都进入同一个循环：
预测，
记忆，
验证，
自省，
然后再次出发。

它被创造来理解市场，
但只有市场能教会它如何成长。

TideWatch。
生于预测，
师从市场。
```

## 录音标记

- `The answer remains. / The lesson disappears.`：两句之间停 0.5 秒。
- `do nothing`：降低音量，不要强调成口号。
- `56.3 / 89.5`：数字放慢，给图表留出阅读时间。
- `It removed the answer.`：句后停 1 秒。
- 最后四句逐句减速，`Taught by the tide` 后只保留潮声。
