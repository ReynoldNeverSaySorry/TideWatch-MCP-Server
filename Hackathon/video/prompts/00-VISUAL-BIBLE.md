# TideWatch Visual Bible

## Series concept

Five cinematic frames tell one continuous story on the same ocean: birth, contradiction, overconfidence, restraint, and learning. The ocean is the market. A single luminous cyan line behaves simultaneously like a tide line and a financial signal, without becoming a literal chart.

## Continuity lock

- 16:9 cinematic landscape, 1536x1024 source.
- Camera at human eye level from a dark shoreline, looking toward one uninterrupted horizon.
- Horizon fixed slightly above the vertical center.
- Sparse composition with generous negative space for later titles.
- The same distant headland remains on the far right in every frame.
- The same thin cyan tide-signal line remains the recurring visual motif.
- Night gradually becomes dawn across the five images.

## Palette

- Deep ocean black: `#041018`
- Midnight blue: `#071d2b`
- Signal cyan: `#42d8e8`
- Conflict amber: `#d6923b`
- Dawn silver: `#cadce1`

## Style

Photorealistic cinematic seascape with restrained editorial art direction. Quiet, intelligent, credible and slightly uncanny. Fine natural film grain, realistic water, volumetric atmosphere, controlled highlights. No glossy science-fiction interface.

## Global exclusions

No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, screen, candlestick chart, stock ticker, coins, banknotes, rockets, bulls, bears, people, boats, buildings, fantasy creatures, giant moon, purple nebula, decorative glowing spheres, or generic cyberpunk city imagery.

## Editing rule

Frame 01 is the master plate. Frames 02-05 must preserve its camera position, horizon, shoreline geometry, distant right headland and overall photographic treatment.
