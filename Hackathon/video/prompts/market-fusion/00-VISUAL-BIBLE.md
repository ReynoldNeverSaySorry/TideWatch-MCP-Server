# TideWatch Market Fusion Visual Bible

## Creative direction

This series makes the market unmistakable. The ocean remains the narrative world, but financial forces are physically embedded into it: candlesticks rise from water, volume bars continue beneath the surface, red and green order flows become currents, and bull/bear forms emerge from waves.

The result should feel like premium financial mythology, not a brokerage advertisement.

## Continuity lock

- Five frames depict the same financial ocean from the same elevated shoreline viewpoint.
- A dark futuristic exchange skyline sits low on the horizon; architecture stays identical.
- A luminous market grid is visible beneath the transparent water.
- Candlesticks and volume bars are dimensional objects integrated into the sea, never flat overlays.
- Palette moves from midnight toward silver dawn.
- Financial identity must remain legible at thumbnail size.

## Palette

- Market black: `#03080d`
- Exchange navy: `#071725`
- Bullish red: `#e54d4d` (A-share visual convention)
- Bearish green: `#21b889`
- Signal cyan: `#42d8e8`
- Warning gold: `#e0a340`
- Dawn silver: `#c8dbe2`

## Recurring motifs

- Cyan analytical line: TideWatch's observation.
- Gold memory particles: verified historical outcomes.
- Red/green currents: competing bullish and bearish evidence.
- Bull and bear: market regimes, rendered as wave forms or monumental reflections rather than decorative statues.

## Typography rule

Do not ask the image model to render readable labels, percentages or stock codes. Those must be added in editing. Abstract columns of market glyphs and numerical light are allowed when they remain atmospheric and illegible.

## Exclusions

No readable text, logos, watermark, casino chips, piles of money, cryptocurrency symbols, Wall Street flags, traders celebrating, luxury cars, cartoon illustration, purple sci-fi palette, or generic cyberpunk alley.
